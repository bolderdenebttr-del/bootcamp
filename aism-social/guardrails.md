# Content guardrails

Check every post against this list before it goes out. Each rule comes from the full program doc (2026.10.27).

## Never say

- **"Alone" / "ганцаараа" / "бие даан"** about projects. Always say "in teams of 3–4, each student owns a named part."
- **Any promise of admission, scholarships, jobs, internships or placement.** College prep is counseling, not a guarantee.
- **Financial aid / AISM scholarships.** This is paused (10.22). Don't mention it anywhere, social included, until it reopens.
- **Discounts, early-bird pricing, referral codes.** None exist.
- **That the certificate is academic credit, accreditation or grade advancement.** It isn't.
- **"Top tier" or "international"** about coaches/mentors, until the confirmed roster proves it.
- **That AI writes essays.** AI never writes, rewrites or translates application essays.

## Needs consent before posting

- **Coach / mentor / speaker names and photos:** only after their written consent. Confirmed coaches (Gandi, Anar, Esmaeil) still need written OK to be named publicly.
- **Student names and photos:** separate marketing consent. A student who says no keeps their seat.
- **Interview subjects and user testers** in student projects: their identities never appear.
