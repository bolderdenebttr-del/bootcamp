# Post ideas: program first

Ranked by how much each post helps people understand the program and apply before **11.15 23:59**
(19 days from 10.27). Formats are suggestions: carousel = swipe post, reel = short vertical video,
motion = AISM paper/ink/khadag explainer (`aism-motion-explainer` skill).

Check every idea against `guardrails.md`. ⚠️ marks ideas that touch something still pending.

## Tier 1: core program posts (publish before 11.15)

These answer "what is it, who is it for, how do I get in". Without them, nothing else lands.

### 1. Cohort 1 announcement: the whole program on one screen
**Done:** [`posts/01-cohort-1-launch`](posts/01-cohort-1-launch/post.md), a 5-slide carousel plus caption.
- **Hook:** "13 долоо хоног. 24 шууд хичээл. 3 бодит төсөл. 1 Demo Day."
- **Content:** live online in Mongolian; starts 11.20; teams of 3–4; mentor for every team; college prep included; apply by 11.15.
- **Format:** carousel (6–8 slides) + pinned post. This is the anchor every other post links back to.
- ⚠️ Say "Demo Day in February" until 02.20 is approved.

### 2. "Эхлээд чи. Дараа нь AI. Хичээл бүрт." The AI philosophy
- **Hook:** "AI-г эхний өдрөөс ашиглана. Гэхдээ хэзээ ч эхэнд биш."
- **Content:** the four AI roles that grow with you: critic → tutor → builder → teammate. "The more you can check, the more AI does."
- **Format:** motion explainer (signature piece), then a 5-slide carousel version.
- **Why first:** this is what sets AISM apart from "learn ChatGPT" courses. It's already the headline on the website.

### 3. From a given problem to your own startup MVP: the 3-project journey
- **Hook:** "Төсөл 1: асуудлыг бид өгнө. Төсөл 3: асуудлаа чи өөрөө олно."
- **Content:** Project 1 prototype → Project 2 full-stack app with data → Project 3 deployed MVP with real users. One slide each, with the AI role and checkpoint.
- **Format:** carousel, or a 30–45 s reel with a staircase visual.
- Use the approved team wording; never "alone".

### 4. "Код мэдэх шаардлагагүй": who it's for
- **Hook:** "Нэг ч мөр код мэдэхгүй байж эхэлнэ. Энэ нь зориуд."
- **Content:** grades 11–12, university years 1–2, gap year (16–20). What you need: a computer, stable internet, 8–10 hours a week. Mention the exceptions briefly (Mongolian students abroad, strong 10th graders).
- **Format:** carousel with a "Is this for me?" checklist.

### 5. What we actually look for (and what we don't)
- **Hook:** "Бид код, англи хэлийг оноонд оруулдаггүй."
- **Content:** the 5 criteria: you notice problems, you start things, you don't quit, you work well with people, you have your own voice.
- **Format:** carousel, one criterion per slide, with a one-line "what this looks like" example.
- **Why:** it removes the biggest barrier ("I'm not technical enough") and attracts the right students.

### 6. How to apply in 20 minutes
- **Hook:** "20 минут. 4 богино хариулт. 60–90 секундын видео. 48 цагт хариу."
- **Content:** the form step by step; no money questions; rolling admission, so seats go in order of arrival; deadline 11.15 23:59.
- **Format:** screen-recording reel + story with the link sticker.

### 7. "Your 60–90 second video": tips post
- **Hook:** "Төгс байх хэрэггүй. Чи өөрөө байх хэрэгтэй."
- **Content:** what a good application video shows (a problem you noticed, something you started) and what doesn't matter (production quality, perfect English).
- **Format:** reel. Lowers drop-off at the hardest step of the form.

### 8. A week inside AISM
- **Hook:** "Мягмар 19:00. Бямба 13:00. Хооронд нь юу болдог вэ?"
- **Content:** session structure (new concept with the whole class → team breakout with mentors), weekly team mission, 30-minute mentor meeting, optional Thursday workshops.
- **Format:** carousel or "timeline" reel.
- ⚠️ Don't print "90 minutes" or "3 hours live" until session length is approved.

### 9. College prep is built in
- **Hook:** "Код, AI, бас их сургуулийн бэлтгэл. Нэг хөтөлбөрт."
- **Content:** three 1:1 sessions, essay feedback, balanced school list, scholarship guide in Mongolian, interview prep, recommendation letter from the lead instructor (conditions apply). Use the approved public line.
- **Format:** carousel aimed at 12th graders and parents.
- Never promise admission. Say "AI never writes your essay."

### 10. For parents: "Таны хүүхэд AI-г аль хэдийн хэрэглэж байгаа."
- **Hook:** the approved parent line, word for word.
- **Content:** the 17% study (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025): unguarded AI hurt learning, a guarded tutor mostly didn't. How AISM checks every week (3 lines from each PR). Safeguarding: no unrecorded 1:1s with under-18s, official channels only, guardians at the opening session.
- **Format:** carousel + Facebook long post (parents are on FB).

### 11. What you walk away with
- **Hook:** "13 долоо хоногийн дараа чамд юу үлдэх вэ?"
- **Content:** 3 team projects with public links and GitHub repos, user research evidence, Demo Day recording, college counseling, a recommendation letter (if conditions are met), the certificate (state that it's not academic credit).
- **Format:** carousel, "portfolio" visual.

### 12. Deadline countdown series
- **Dates:** 11.08 ("1 долоо хоног"), 11.13 ("2 өдөр"), 11.15 morning ("Өнөөдөр 23:59")
- **Content:** the short version of #1 + link. Rolling admission means earlier is better.
- **Format:** stories + one feed post each.
