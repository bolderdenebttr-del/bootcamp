## The AI thread

- AI is in every session from L1 to Demo Day, but **never first**. Order: you → AI → you again.
- Four roles that grow with the student's ability to check AI:
  1. **Critic** (L1): argues with your problem, tree, scope. Produces nothing for you.
  2. **Tutor** (L4): explains and gives hints. You write every line.
  3. **Builder** (after passing the L10 code-reading test): writes code. You explain every line before it goes in the repo.
  4. **Teammate** (L14 and Project 3): works inside the product and does multi-step tasks. You test, steer and own the result.
- **L10 code-reading test = builder license.** 10 minutes: explain 20 unfamiliar lines and find a planted bug. Not passing is not a punishment: you stay in tutor mode, work an extra week with your mentor and retake before 01.05.
- **Core rule:** what you can't explain doesn't go in the project (code, AI-sourced facts, AI output in the product).
- **AI log:** one line for each AI use that changed the work. It later feeds retros, Demo Day, college essays and rec letters.
- **Weekly check:** mentors pick 3 lines from each student's PR and ask them to explain. "We don't police the tool, we test the student."
- **"No AI here" is a valid answer**, and it is asked at L11, L15 and L17.
- **Evidence:** in a study of about 1,000 Turkish high schoolers, the group using unguarded ChatGPT later scored 17% lower on a no-AI exam; a guarded tutor mostly removed the harm (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025).

## Signature moments (good for content)

| L16 | Teams find their own problem: 15 real interviews in 3 days. |
| L17 | Investor Day: Approved / Shrink scope / Rethink. |
| L20 | Debug Day: teachers don't write code, they only ask questions. |
| L21 | MVP goes live, then 10+ real people test it over Tsagaan Sar. "Watch, don't help." |
| L23 | A 4th UI state: "AI answered wrong". |
| L24 | Demo Day. Each team shows one moment AI was wrong and how they caught it. Each student hands in "My AI rules", 5 in their own words. |

**Demo Day judging:** real problem, backed by research 30% · solution fit 25% · it works 20% · improved from user feedback 15% · presentation 10%. Code quality is deliberately not a criterion.

## Safeguarding (trust content for parents)

- No AISM adult meets a student under 18 alone without a recording.
- Decisions and payment notices go to both the student and the guardian.
- Staff use official channels only (email, coordinator's Telegram); no personal social accounts.
- Every mentor, coach and speaker passes a reference check and signs a code of conduct.
- Guardians join the first 20 minutes of the opening session.
