# Positioning and messaging · Cohort 1

Built on the founder's draft (handwritten, 10.28). The draft's story stays; this file sharpens the wording, adds the proof, and turns it into lines we can reuse.

## The draft, as written

> **Main msg:** Are teenagers and young adults getting dumber because of AI? Are we getting dumber because of AI? Is AI making us stupid?
> **Answer:** It's not the AI or technology. It's because no one taught us how in the first place. Everyone, literally everyone, is going through this.
> **Main take:** But so few of us act on this very efficiently and make the most out of it. Like Zuckerberg, Musk, Altman, etc.
> **CTA:** Everyone possesses this trait if prompted correctly when beginning. That's why we exist. We will think first. Then we will tackle the problems we find valuable to solve with AI. We will ask questions, ponder, get creative, try, test, build, iterate, think, but most importantly: learn to optimize, solve problems and think by leveraging AI.
> **Plus:** experienced mentors (global + local), AI tools, college prep (not an extensive one, compact yet enough), Demo Day, real experience.

## The story in five beats

| Beat | Job | Line (MN) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Tension | Stop the scroll with a fear everyone shares | **AI биднийг тэнэгрүүлж байна уу?** |
| 2. Reframe | Take the blame off AI and off the viewer | **Асуудал нь AI биш. Бидэнд хэн ч зааж өгөөгүй л юм.** Хүн бүр яг одоо үүнийг туулж байна. |
| 3. Proof | Show the reframe is true, not an opinion | 1,000 орчим сурагчтай туршилт: хамгаалалтгүй AI ашигласан бүлэг AI-гүй шалгалтад 17%-иар доогуур. Зөв ашигласан бүлэгт энэ хор нөлөө ихэвчлэн арилсан. (Bastani et al., PNAS 2025) |
| 4. Gap | Make it aspirational | **Хүн бүр AI хэрэглэдэг. Цөөхөн нь AI-аар бүтээдэг.** |
| 5. Belief + offer | Why AISM exists and what it does | **Хүн бүрт энэ чадвар бий. Зөв эхлэл л хэрэгтэй. Тийм учраас бид байгаа.** Асуудлыг эхлээд олно. Дараа нь AI-аар шийднэ. |

Rule: **every piece that opens with beat 1 resolves with beat 2 in the same piece.** On its own, the question reads as fear-mongering. Answered, it's the brand.

## What changed from the draft, and why

- **"We", not "you".** The hook asks "are *we* getting dumber" (your second line), not "are teenagers". A 17-year-old who feels accused scrolls past. One who feels included stays.
- **Zuckerberg, Musk and Altman come out.** Three reasons:
  1. They split opinion.
  2. They're distant from a UB 11th grader.
  3. Meta screens ads that show famous faces, because scams use them, so those ads risk rejection or delay.
  The "gap" line (beat 4) does the same job without borrowing anyone's name: *few people build with AI*. The proof comes from AISM's own students at Demo Day.
- **"If prompted correctly" becomes the campaign's wordplay:** **"AI-д зөв prompt хэрэгтэй. Хүнд ч бас."** This is your idea, tightened. Students who use AI get it instantly.
- **The verb list becomes the program.** Each verb in your CTA is a real session, so the manifesto is proof, not poetry:

  | Verb | Where it happens |
  |---|---|
  | Асууна (ask) | L9: interviews with real people |
  | Эргэцүүлнэ (ponder) | L2: problem tree on paper, before any code |
  | Оролдоно (try) | L4–L7: you write every line, AI as tutor |
  | Бүтээнэ (build) | L14: frontend + database + AI in the product |
  | Туршина (test) | L21: 10+ real people use your MVP |
  | Сайжруулна (iterate) | L22: sort the feedback, fix what matters |
  | Тайлбарлана (explain) | L10 code test, L24 Demo Day: "what you can't explain doesn't go in" |

- **"Compact yet enough" college prep** is the right internal idea, but publicly we lead with what's included: 45 минутын гурван 1:1 уулзалт, эсээний санал, сургуулийн жагсаалт, зөвлөмж захидал. Never call it "not extensive", and never promise admission.
- **"Global mentors" → "Монгол болон гадаадад ажилладаг коучууд"**, the site's wording, until the roster is public.

## Core script: the 45-second founder reel

This is the campaign in one piece (Day 1). Every other piece is a slice of it.

```
[0–3с]   AI биднийг тэнэгрүүлж байна уу?
[3–10с]  Даалгавраа AI-аар хийлгэдэг. Эсээгээ AI-аар бичүүлдэг. Заримдаа бодохоо больчихсон юм шиг санагддаг.
[10–20с] Асуудал нь AI биш. Бидэнд хэн ч үүнийг яаж зөв ашиглахыг зааж өгөөгүй л юм. Хүн бүр яг одоо үүнийг туулж байна.
[20–30с] Хүн бүр AI хэрэглэдэг. Харин цөөхөн нь AI-аар бүтээдэг. Ялгаа нь: тэд эхлээд боддог.
[30–40с] Хүн бүрт энэ чадвар бий. Зөв эхлэл л хэрэгтэй. Тийм учраас AI School of Mongolia байгаа.
         Асуудлыг эхлээд олно. Дараа нь AI-аар шийднэ.
[40–45с] Cohort 1-ийн элсэлт 11.15 хүртэл. Код мэдэх шаардлагагүй. Холбоос bio-д.
```

Film it as one take, straight to camera, with captions burned in (most people watch muted). Then cut the 10-second hook alone as a second version for ads.
